The reason behind the work

Why We Build

We believe technology should solve problems people actually experience.

Instead of building for trends, we look for everyday challenges worth making simpler — including the ones we’ve experienced ourselves. Then we build, listen and improve with the people those solutions are for.

Our Nordic perspective keeps the work practical, honest and grounded in trust. Curiosity helps us ask better questions; progress means launching, listening and improving.

Human first Useful technology should earn its place in someone’s day: less friction, more confidence, and a little more room for what matters.

Ideas becoming useful things

What We’re Building

Different everyday problems, one shared belief: thoughtful tools can make a real difference.

Launching
J

JobHawk

A steadier way through the job search.

An AI-powered career coach to help people improve their CVs, write applications and navigate the search for their next opportunity.

The problem: Finding work can be confusing, lonely and hard to know how to approach.

In Development
S

SpotHawk

Make more of the chargers already out there.

Peer-to-peer EV charger sharing that helps drivers find a place to charge and lets homeowners share their private charger.

The problem: Convenient charging can be difficult to find when you’re away from home.

Beta
B

BadgeHawk

Make your team identity unmistakably yours.

A creative toolkit for generating professional AI-made emblems, logos and gaming identities for teams and communities.

The problem: Small teams deserve identity design that feels as considered as the people behind it.

Prototype
M

MyFluffy

A little more peace of mind for pet people.

Connected pet technology exploring how thoughtful tracking and health insights can support the bond between people and their animals.

The problem: It’s hard to care for what you can’t easily understand or keep track of.
